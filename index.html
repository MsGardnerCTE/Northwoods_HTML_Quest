Opening vs. Closing Tags

An opening tag starts an HTML element, such as <h1>.

A closing tag ends it and includes a slash, such as </h1>.

🔤

Headings

Headings are section titles that organize a webpage and make it easier to read.

<h1><h2><h3><h4><h5><h6>
📋

Lists

<ol> creates a numbered list. <ul> creates a bulleted list.

<li> means list item and belongs inside either type of list.
